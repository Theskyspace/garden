Not a dictation app — a voice layer. Once the Mac understands you locally, typing is the
least interesting thing it can do with that understanding. "Add milk to my todo list"
lands in Reminders. "Open Slack" opens Slack. Dictate the angry email, say "refine it
professionally," send the version that won't get you in trouble.

The constraint set is the product: on-device [Parakeet](https://github.com/NVIDIA/NeMo)
on the Neural Engine, no account, no server that ever hears you, and a one-time price
instead of monthly rent for software running on your own silicon.

The whole thing is being built in the open — the bugs, the design traps, the 2 AM tester
calls. See [flowstate.crackedbuilder.com](https://flowstate.crackedbuilder.com),
[why I'm building it](/writing/why-im-building-flowstate), and the living
[changelog](/writing/flowstate-changelog).
